Much like hepatitis B, hepatitis C is also a chronic liver infection. It is spread by the hepatitis C virus, or HCV, which is a blood-borne virus. The World Health Organisation estimates that 58 million people suffer from chronic hepatitis C, with 1.5 million new infections annually.
