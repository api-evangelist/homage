Due to the tropical weather conditions and our climate, Singapore is a natural breeding ground for mosquitoes. Besides the inconvenience of itchy mosquito bites and a bit of blood, mosquitoes can also cause dengue fever. While most dengue cases are mild, the virus can be deadly—even causing death in severe cases.
